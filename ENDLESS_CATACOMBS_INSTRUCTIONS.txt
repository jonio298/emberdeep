EMBERDEEP - ENDLESS CATACOMBS (EXPLORATION): DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS
=======================================================================
Read ENDLESS_MODES_COMMON_INSTRUCTIONS.txt first (mode select, character choice, timer, run end, reward tiers, co-op). This file defines
what is specific to the Catacombs mode.

THE FEEL: an endless descent. Each floor is a fresh, bigger, nastier maze. You explore, find the stairs, loot, take risks, and go deeper. The depth
counter and the clock at the top are your score. Where Plains is "survive the swarm", Catacombs is "read the room, plan, and push on".
It is solo or co-op, with your leveled hero or a new one. Progress value for rewards: P = floors reached * 2.


1. RUN STRUCTURE
----------------
- The run starts on FLOOR 1 at 00:00. Reaching the stairs takes you to the next floor (use the existing CC.nextFloor / loadFloor flow, but stay
  in the Endless run: never return to town, never write campaign progress).
- The depth label under the clock reads "FLOOR N". A floor is "reached" the moment you arrive on it.
- There is no final floor. Floors keep generating with a seed derived from the run seed and the floor number (so a retry can be seeded for a
  daily-challenge feature later).
- Every 5th floor is a BOSS FLOOR (5, 10, 15 ...). Boss floors have a smaller arena, one boss, add waves, and the stairs appear when the boss
  falls. Between boss floors the descent gets steadily harder.
- Every 5 floors the BIOME changes (see section 4), the lighting gets darker, and the creatures get scarier.


2. FLOOR GENERATION (reuse dungeon.js)
--------------------------------------
- Use the existing Catacombs generator (CC.Dungeon, the same one the campaign floors use) with these depth-driven parameters:
    size:        grows slowly with depth, capped (a late floor is a big maze but never so big that finding the stairs is tedious)
    rooms:       more rooms and more branches with depth, a few dead ends that hold rewards
    stairs:      placed far from the spawn (the "explore" part), usually behind at least one choke point or guarded room
    enemy packs: count and size grow with depth; some packs are asleep until you enter their room (stealth lets you avoid them)
    chests:      1-3 per floor, deeper floors add a "vault" with a mini-guardian
    props/hazards: spike traps, collapsing floors, poison pools and fire vents appear from floor 4, with clear telegraphs
- Fog of war and the minimap: the minimap reveals only explored areas. Picking up a "map fragment" reveals more of the floor. Keep the
  existing minimap and quest-arrow logic working; the stairs get an arrow ONLY after the player has seen them or used a fragment.
- Add a "floor goal ring" at the stairs that opens only when the floor's guardian condition is met on boss floors.


3. EXPLORATION AND RISK/REWARD (what makes it an exploration mode)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- SHRINES: optional altars on some floors. The player may pay (health, gold, or a curse) for a boon (damage, speed, loot luck). A shrine is
  a choice, never forced. Curses make later enemies tougher but raise reward luck. Keep a small, readable list of active shrines in the HUD.
- SECRET ROOMS: cracked walls (existing Breakables) hide treasure rooms with an elite or a trapped chest.
- KEYS and LOCKED DOORS: a key on the floor opens a vault door; the vault has a guaranteed good item for the run (run-only, see common file).
- ELITE ROOMS: a marked door with a stronger pack and a better chest. Skippable.
- LANTERN: the player has a light radius; oil pickups extend it. Darkness is part of the danger (see section 4).
- THE HUNT (pressure so the player keeps moving): after 90 seconds on a floor a WARDEN starts hunting the player, a tough roaming enemy that
  is slower than the player but never stops, shown as a purple off-screen arrow (the existing boss-arrow system works for this). A second
  Warden is added every further 60 seconds. Taking the stairs removes them. This keeps a "go or fight" tension without a hard timer.
- LOOT inside a run is run-only (see the common file). It exists to make the hero stronger for the descent, not to replace the reward.


4. BIOMES, DARKNESS AND SCARINESS (every 5 floors)
--------------------------------------------------
  Floors 1-4    "Upper Crypts"     torches, stone, skeletons, bats, rats, slimes (the familiar enemies).
  Floors 5-9    "Ossuary"          bone piles, ghouls, necromancer casters, bone golems; boss at 5 (a Bone Colossus).
  Floors 10-14  "Flooded Vaults"   water hazards, drowned things, eels, spectral shots; boss at 10 (the Drowned Queen family).
  Floors 15-19  "Hollow Halls"     eyes in the dark, teleporting horrors (Veyr-class), mirrors, scarecrow flocks; boss at 15.
  Floors 20-24  "Flesh Warrens"    patchwork shamblers, grinning masks, living walls; boss at 20 (a Patchwork Horror).
  Floors 25+    "The Deep"         everything above, nightmare variants (red eyes, +size, new telegraphs), darker than dark.
Darkness per biome (ambient light): 100%, 80%, 62%, 48%, 38%, 30%. The hero's lantern always lights a radius of at least 110 px.
Fire, spell glows, the Smoke Bomb and lightning stay bright and look better in the dark. Telegraphs, pickups, the hero and the minimap must
stay readable at every depth (additive glow, outlines, shape and motion, not colour alone). Add a "reduce darkness" accessibility option
that caps the darkness at biome 3.
Each biome shift gets a small banner ("THE OSSUARY") and a music change, then returns to normal play.


5. ENEMY SCALING (depth, not hero level)
----------------------------------------
- Base from CC.Enemies.TYPES; multiply by a depth curve: health x(1 + 0.18*floor), damage x(1 + 0.10*floor), speed x(1 + 0.02*floor, capped +35%).
  Put the numbers in CC.Endless.TUNING.catacombs. They apply together with the difficulty-mode multipliers (CC.Difficulty), so never apply
  those again inside the director.
- Elites: from floor 3, one pack per floor includes an elite (glowing outline, +100% health, a better drop). From floor 10, two.
- Boss floors: reuse the campaign bosses and mini-bosses (Bone Colossus, Drowned Queen, Veyr, Patchwork Horror, Grinning Mask). Their health
  and damage take the same depth multipliers. Bosses stay immune to confusion, and their off-screen arrow shows.
- Co-op: x1.7 enemy count per pack and x1.25 health; the same floor layout for both players.


6. BUILD PLAN (after the shared endless.js exists)
--------------------------------------------------
 1. CatacombsDirector: starts a run on floor 1, advances floors, calls the existing generator with the depth parameters, applies the
    enemy depth multipliers, shows the floor label, never touches campaign progress.
 2. Floor flow: stairs -> next floor -> floor label banner -> continue. The clock keeps running during the transition animation only if the world updates.
 3. Boss floors: pick the boss for the biome, add the arena rules, open the stairs on its death.
 4. Exploration features in this order: chests and vaults -> map fragments and fog -> shrines -> keys and locked doors -> secret rooms -> the Warden hunt.
 5. Biomes: darkness levels, palettes, enemy tables, props, music.
 6. Co-op: host generates each floor from the shared seed, both players arrive together (wait for both at the stairs or a timed regroup of 8 seconds),
    revive rules from the common file.
 7. Balance log (floors per minute, damage taken per floor, deaths by enemy type, time per floor) printed to the console and stored in the run
    summary. Tune TUNING from real runs.


7. TESTS AND ACCEPTANCE
-----------------------
Automated:
 - Floor generation: for floors 1, 5, 10, 25 and 60 and several seeds, the map is connected from spawn to stairs, has the expected chests, and the
   stairs are reachable without solid tiles in the way; enemy counts follow the depth table.
 - Depth scaling: enemy stats at floor N equal base x the formulas above (within rounding), once (not doubled by difficulty).
 - Boss floors: the stairs only open after the boss dies; the right boss spawns for the biome.
 - Run end/retry: all floor, enemy, shrine, key and Warden state is cleared; campaign dungeon state is untouched.
Manual / browser:
 - Descend to floor 6 and floor 11; screenshot each biome. Light level must visibly drop and stay readable.
 - A shrine, a key door, a secret room and the Warden arrow all work, and hold-to-charge spells, Smoke Bomb and the boss arrows work in the maze.
 - A 20-minute run keeps the frame rate steady (cleanup of old floors, no leaked fx or enemies).
Definition of done: starting Endless Catacombs gives an endless chain of different floors with rising danger and darkness, a top-centre clock and floor
number, boss floors every 5, optional risk/reward rooms, a Warden that keeps the player moving, a run summary on death, and a reward whose
rarity rises with floors reached (see the common file), in solo and in co-op.
