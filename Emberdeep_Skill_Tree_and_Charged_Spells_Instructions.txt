EMBERDEEP — SKILL TREE AND PAIRED SPELL REDESIGN
DETAILED DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS
Prepared for implementation in repository: https://github.com/jonio298/emberdeep
Reference code inspected: main branch at commit 0fa56323 (2026-11-24). Re-check HEAD before editing.

======================================================================
1. GOAL AND NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN RULE
======================================================================

Redesign Emberdeep's four character classes to eliminate repetitive active skills,
create distinctive build paths, and ensure EVERY active spell has:
  (A) a quick REGULAR/TAP version, and
  (B) a stronger, visually more dramatic CHARGED/HOLD version.

CRITICAL RULE: The charged spell MUST be a logical, recognizable upgrade of the
same regular spell. It may hit harder, last longer, reach farther, add projectiles,
enlarge an area, improve control, or add a related secondary effect. It must NOT
turn into an unrelated ability. A player should immediately recognize the same
spell being empowered by charging it.

Implement this rule for ALL FIVE active abilities for each of the four classes
(20 abilities; 40 paired spell behaviors), including abilities unlocked after
level 4. Do not limit hold-to-charge to the two original starting spells.

Keep Emberdeep's fast, intuitive, crunchy arcade combat, responsive mobile
controls, visible projectiles, impact effects, satisfying combat feedback, and
pixel-art visual style. Avoid performance-heavy effects that obscure enemies
or the player, particularly on mobile.

The separately selected HOLD-ATK charge specials (Earthsplitter, Lightning
Spear, Soul Spear, etc.) are a different system. Keep those independent from
ordinary spell tap/hold pairing. Do not replace or confuse the Wizard's
existing yellow lightning-spear special with Icicle Cannon.

2. RELEVANT CURRENT SYSTEMS / INSPECTION NOTES
======================================================================

Current files to inspect before editing:
  abilities.js       Five active abilities per class; ability definitions,
                     unlocked levels, slots, effects, mini-upgrade trees.
  spell_charge.js    Hold-to-aim and charged versions, currently restricted
                     mainly to the original spell1/spell2 plus dash.
  classes.js         Original spell casts, projectiles, damage, statuses,
                     combat and projectile collisions.
  skilltree.js       Shared passive nodes, class mastery, HOLD-ATK specials.
  charge.js          Independent HOLD-ATK special abilities.
  ui.js              Skill screen and charge special selection.
  vfx.js             Combat effects and sprite rendering.
  main.js            HUD, inputs, rendering, update loop.
  save_game.js       Character skill ranks, slots, state serialization.
  multiplayer.js    Replication of spell visuals/partner effects where applicable.
  tests/             Existing automated combat, charge, and lifecycle tests.

Current structure:
  - 5 ordinary abilities/class at levels 1, 4, 8, 16, 26.
  - 4 equippable ordinary ability slots (Q/E/R/T / mobile buttons).
  - Each ability currently shares generic Power, Reach, Haste, Effect, Mastery
    upgrades; many upgrades merely increase damage, radius or cooldown.
  - Three common passive columns: Offense, Defense, Utility, plus linear
    class mastery nodes and repeatable Training.
  - Separately selected HOLD-ATK specials unlock at levels 1, 10, 20, 30.
  - Current spell_charge.js defines charged versions of initial spells and
    dash but not all three additional unlockable abilities.
  - Current charged and regular original spells have SEPARATE cooldowns.

Change the spell-ability model so every equipped spell can resolve its own
regular and charged cast through the same slot, while preserving the separate
HOLD-ATK special system and existing charge-dash behavior.

3. UNIVERSAL TAP / HOLD INTERACTION AND UI
======================================================================

TAP:
  - Tap/release BEFORE 0.25 seconds: execute the regular spell.
  - No charge preview should appear on a quick tap.
  - Apply standard mana cost and normal spell cooldown.

HOLD:
  - At >=0.25 seconds, enter the charged targeting state.
  - Show a subtle spell-specific transparent preview (cone, line, radius,
    single-target outline, etc.), and growing charging visual/audio.
  - At approximately 0.90 seconds total hold, reach full charge.
  - Continue holding may maintain full charge without automatic firing.
  - On release, execute the charged version at the current aim location.
  - During an incomplete charge after 0.25 seconds, scale charged power
    smoothly according to actual time held; don't generate both tap and
    charged casts from the same press.
  - Charged versions cost MORE mana and have LONGER cooldowns than taps.
  - Initial guide: roughly +50% to +80% mana and ~1.5x to 2x cooldown,
    tuned per ability. Avoid enormous damage increases when the charge
    adds multiple hits, crowd control or sustained area effects.
  - Regular and charged versions should use ONE shared cooldown per
    spell (charged cast writes the longer duration to the SAME cooldown).
    This supersedes the existing separate p.cd and p.cdC arrangement for
    spell abilities. Do not accidentally create a second free tap cast.
  - If a hold is cancelled or mana is unavailable, do not consume mana
    or trigger hidden damage. Communicate cooldown/unavailable state.

MOBILE:
  - Preserve the existing thumb-drag aim controls, especially SE aiming.
  - Grow the held ability button subtly after the 0.25s threshold; keep
    an internal aim knob so the thumb can clearly steer the attack.
  - Targeting indicators should be semi-transparent, understandable and
    anchored in the world. Spell visuals must not cover the player.
  - Support diagonal casts and edge-of-screen drag amplification.

PC:
  - Apply the same semantics on equipped Q/E/R/T keys, with mouse or
    current directional targeting consistent with existing game behavior.

HUD / SKILLS SCREEN:
  - Show BOTH TAP and HOLD descriptions for EVERY unlocked ability.
  - Show spell-specific preview type, normal/charged mana and cooldown.
  - Clearly distinguish independent HOLD-ATK special attacks.
  - Distinct charge VFX and stronger audio must match the base spell's
    elemental/martial identity.

4. WIZARD — REPLACE CHAIN LIGHTNING WITH ICICLE CANNON
   (HIGH PRIORITY; FULL DETAILED SPECIFICATION)
======================================================================

Replace the Wizard's level-8 ordinary active ability "Chain Lightning" with:

   NAME: ICICLE CANNON
   ID: Prefer preserve existing ability ID "bolt" for save/slot migration,
       but change display name, kind, behavior, effect upgrades, and VFX.
   TYPE: Frost / physical-looking ice shards / piercing / limited splash.
   COLORS: Crisp pale blue, cyan, white-blue highlights, ice fragments.
   INTENT: Powerful visible ice projectiles, NOT an invisible line hit, NOT
           homing chain lightning, NOT a generic frost ring.

----------------------------
4A. REGULAR TAP: PIERCING ICICLE
----------------------------

  - A single LONG, pointed, visibly distinct icicle shoots from the
    Wizard's staff in the selected direction.
  - Fast, straight-moving physical projectile with visible ice trail.
  - Pierce UP TO THREE DISTINCT ENEMIES. The same projectile may hit each
    enemy only once and MUST terminate after the third valid direct hit
    or when it reaches max range / solid terrain.
  - At EACH direct hit, produce a SMALL explosive ice splinter impact that
    deals LIMITED SPLASH DAMAGE to nearby other enemies.
  - Piercing must remain visible: the icicle should pass through the first
    and second enemies and continue toward the third, not disappear early.
  - Small glacial chip effects, brief cold hit response; don't turn it
    into a room-clearing AoE spell.
  - Wall collision: sharp glassy ice shatter, then remove projectile.
  - No visually impossible damage to offscreen enemies or behind walls.

  STARTING BALANCE TARGETS (adjust with playtests):
    - Projectile count: 1
    - Max enemy pierces: 3 distinct direct hits
    - Travel distance: approximately 300 world pixels
    - Direction: aim direction / target line
    - Splash: small radius around EACH direct hit (starting 14-22 px)
    - Base mana: 24
    - Base cooldown: 5 seconds

----------------------------
4B. CHARGED HOLD: ICICLE CANNON / ICICLE BARRAGE
----------------------------

  - At 0.25s, reveal a readable, semi-transparent BLUE CONE preview.
    The cone rotates with the player's thumb/mouse/directional aim.
  - During charging, multiple needle-sharp icicles assemble visibly in
    front of the Wizard's staff. They grow, brighten, vibrate and gather
    ice mist/crystal particles as charge increases.
  - On release, FIRE ACTUAL VISIBLE ICICLE PROJECTILES in a spreading
    cone — an ice SHOTGUN / CANNON, not a single flat cone damage shape.
  - Each icicle MUST travel independently, pointing in its movement
    direction, with a sharp leading point and an ice particle trail.
  - Suggested initial firing pattern: 9 icicles in 3 distinct fast waves
    of 3, with staggered offsets across an approximately 55-degree cone.
    Waves should be visible as they burst out sequentially rather than
    spawning all nine simultaneously and looking like one blue flash.
  - Each shard should have its own collision and SMALL splash of ice
    fragments on impact. Charged shards may pierce one enemy at base
    rank (upgrades can improve this); don't apply every shard's full
    damage repeatedly to the same large target without a cap.
  - On hitting a monster, the icicle visibly EMBEDS / STICKS INTO the
    monster's body at the angle it entered. This is important. The
    embedded shards move with the monster for approximately 0.8-1.2s.
    Shards then crack, fall away or shatter; on death they can burst
    into ice pieces and vanish. Use a per-enemy cap on embedded shards.
  - Distinct impact sound: crisp crunchy piercing, glass/ice crack on
    impact, followed by little clinks when shards fall or shatter.
  - The charged cannon should FEEL stronger than the single icicle
    through its number of projectiles, area coverage, impact and visual
    feedback while still fitting the fast Emberdeep arcade rhythm.

  STARTING BALANCE TARGETS:
    - Total icicles: 9, over 3 short bursts of 3
    - Targeting: cone, ~55 degrees wide
    - Max travel distance: ~225 world pixels
    - Direct hits: up to 1 distinct enemy per shard by default
    - Splash: small per-shard hit effect, tightly capped
    - Charged mana: 42
    - Charged cooldown: 10 seconds (shared with regular tap cast)
    - Suggested full charge: ~0.9s total hold
    - Against bosses, cap aggregate damage across the full volley and
      never multiply damage accidentally by overlapping splash explosions.

----------------------------
4C. DAMAGE, COLLISION AND EMBEDDED-ICE RULES
----------------------------

  - Store a hitSet (or comparable list) PER PROJECTILE, not globally.
  - Count unique direct-hit enemies; stop regular icicle on hit #3.
  - Splash must not re-hit the direct target on the same impact unless
    deliberately balanced, and should not trigger itself recursively.
  - Same enemy might be struck by multiple charged shards, but per-volley
    damage falloff, a local hit interval or total cap must prevent absurd
    damage at point-blank range and against large bosses.
  - Apply frost slow consistently with upgrades (see below) without
    per-hit infinite stun stacking. Bosses must follow existing
    crowd-control resistance rules.
  - Distinguish projectile state, impact state and embedded-shard state.
    An embedded shard is a temporary visual attachment, NOT an active
    projectile and should not keep dealing collision damage.
  - Embedded shards follow enemy world position, rotation/direction as
    applicable; clean up on death, despawn, area transitions, saves,
    scene reset, and when an entity is recycled.
  - Use pooled/limited VFX and a max embedded shard count per enemy.
  - Multiplayer: replicate launch effects and impacts so remote players
    see the cast; simulate gameplay damage only on the authoritative side
    of the existing system to prevent duplicate hits.
  - Render projectiles and embedded shards ABOVE fog as appropriate so
    the player can understand the attack during the Pond boss encounter.

----------------------------
4D. ICICLE CANNON UNIQUE SKILL UPGRADES
----------------------------

  1. DEEP PIERCING
     Tap: maximum pierces grows from 3 to 4 at an earned rank.
     Charge: each individual icicle may pierce one additional target.

  2. SPLINTERBURST
     Tap: slightly wider splash on each pierced impact.
     Charge: larger ice splinters erupt from embedded shard impacts.

  3. FROSTBITE / GLACIAL PIN
     Tap: pierced targets suffer a short movement slow.
     Charge: sufficient shard hits apply stronger slow / brief freeze,
     respecting boss CC rules and stun limits.

  4. SHATTER MASTERY
     Tap: deals increased damage to frozen targets.
     Charge: embedded icicles break into a final small splash when
     the target dies; cap these explosions to avoid exponential damage.

  5. ABSOLUTE ZERO (CAPSTONE)
     Requires deep investment in Icicle Cannon. Targets hit repeatedly by
     charged shards can briefly freeze; a later ice hit produces a clear
     shatter burst with capped bonus damage. No infinite freeze loops.

These are behavior-changing upgrades; any Power/Reach/Haste numerical ranks
should be secondary to the visible and tactical evolution of the spell.

IMPORTANT: The Wizard already has Frost Nova -> charged Icicle Barrage as a
separate starting-spell design in the inspected code. Avoid making two
indistinguishable ice barrages. Under THIS plan, change charged Frost Nova to
GLACIAL NOVA, a larger radial wave + second pulse + erupting ground ice, while
Icicle Cannon uniquely fires FORWARD-FACING projectiles in a CONE.

5. COMPLETE 20-SPELL TAP / CHARGE PAIRINGS
======================================================================

SWORDSMAN — deliberate impact, precision, defense, and combo execution
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1) SHIELD BASH
   Tap: Quick close-range frontal shield hit that stuns.
   Charge: SHIELD BREAKER — visibly wind up the same bash, rush/strike
           farther forward, stagger several foes and reduce enemy armor.
   Why related: A stronger shield bash, not a separate magical attack.

2) WHIRLWIND
   Tap: One fast 360-degree sword spin.
   Charge: BLADE STORM — the same spinning attack extends into three
           clearly animated successive spins, with a modest enemy pull.
   Why related: Charging extends the existing spinning technique.

3) LEAP SLAM
   Tap: Short leap followed by local shockwave on landing.
   Charge: EARTHSHAKER — longer aimed leap and more powerful impact with
           cracks/fissures travelling outward from the landing.
   Why related: More committed leap and slam.

4) BLADE WAVE
   Tap: One narrow piercing blue crescent fired forward.
   Charge: GREAT CLEAVE — wider, thicker crescent, stronger stagger and
           longer travel, keeping the same projectile shape and direction.
   Why related: Intensified sword wave.

5) WAR CRY
   Tap: Short temporary buff to damage and movement speed.
   Charge: BATTLE ROAR — visibly louder/larger war cry that extends the
           buff and additionally improves damage resistance for a while.
   Why related: Same rallying cry empowered, not a new damage spell.

Swordsman anti-duplication: WHIRLWIND is the one spinning skill. Do not make
other unrelated charged skills into additional circular spins. Ensure the
independent HOLD-ATK specials likewise do not become redundant chains of
spinning moves; consider replacing independent Blade Storm with a parry/
shield-counter special, and Sky Cleaver with a focused overhead finisher.

WIZARD — elemental projectiles, control, gravity and magical defense
-------------------------------------------------------------------

1) FIREBALL
   Tap: Fire one exploding fireball.
   Charge: INFERNO ORB — an obviously LARGER, slower fireball with a
           stronger impact explosion and lingering flames.

2) FROST NOVA
   Tap: Circular frost wave around the Wizard that damages/slows.
   Charge: GLACIAL NOVA — enlarged circular frost wave, followed by a
           second ice pulse and jagged ground ice from the same wave.
   Note: This REPLACES existing charged Frost Nova's 'Icicle Barrage'
         so it is distinct from Icicle Cannon.

3) ICICLE CANNON (replaces Chain Lightning)
   Tap: ONE sharp icicle projectile, pierce <=3 enemies with small splash
        on each direct hit.
   Charge: Forward CONE of several independently flying icicles, visibly
           fired as a cannon, with shards physically sticking into enemies.
   Complete design details in section 4 are mandatory.

4) ARCANE ORB
   Tap: Slow, powerful orb projectile travelling through foes.
   Charge: GRAVITY ORB — an enlarged version of that same orb; drags
           foes slightly toward itself and detonates at the end.

5) ARCANE WARD (replaces ordinary Blink, as Wizard already has dash
   teleportation)
   Tap: Brief small magical protection shield centered on Wizard.
   Charge: ARCANE FORTRESS — larger/longer protective shield that
           absorbs more damage and releases a shockwave when exhausted.
   Keep existing dash teleport mechanics intact.

NECROMANCER — summons, draining, bone magic, corpse power, curses
----------------------------------------------------------------

1) RAISE SKELETON
   Tap: Raise two ordinary skeleton warriors (respect cap).
   Charge: BONE GUARDIAN — concentrate the same summoning into ONE
           large, tougher skeletal guardian with visibly heavier attacks.
   No uncontrolled additional skeleton population.

2) LIFE DRAIN
   Tap: Focus a green spectral life-drain beam on one enemy.
   Charge: SOUL SIPHON — intensify SAME beam; a primary heavy channel
           branches to nearby targets for sustained but capped drain.

3) BONE SPEAR
   Tap: Fire one pointed bone projectile through a straight line.
   Charge: IMPALING LANCE — much larger bone spear with greater piercing
           and a burst of splinters when it finally hits terrain/expires.

4) CORPSE BLAST
   Tap: Explode one nearby usable corpse; damage surrounding enemies.
   Charge: CORPSE CHAIN — larger original blast propagates to up to two
           nearby corpses in a short visible sequence, with falloff.
   Engineering note: If the current game lacks usable retained corpse
   objects, implement safe, time-limited corpse tokens or adapt current
   corpse/rot mechanics; do not silently allow dead enemies to be hit
   again or rely on non-existent references. Define living-target fallback
   explicitly so the spell remains usable when corpses are absent.

5) HEX OF WEAKNESS
   Tap: Short-range curse reducing foe damage/speed.
   Charge: WITHERING CURSE — stronger and wider version of the same
           curse, with a short damage-over-time/decay component.

Necromancer anti-duplication: RAISE SKELETON is the only dedicated active
summoning spell. Revise/remove summon additions from Grave Choir, Bone
Tide, Army of the Dead and redundant passives. Preserve one cool summon
path, but offer fully viable non-minion plague and soul caster builds.

THIEF — targeting, knives, stealth, traps and toxins
--------------------------------------------------

1) THROWING KNIVES
   Tap: Throw two quick knives toward an aimed enemy.
   Charge: KNIFE BARRAGE — rapid successive knives in a slightly widened
           but still directional aim cone, with separate visible hits.

2) SMOKE BOMB
   Tap: Throw smoke that blinds/confuses enemies and enables backstabs.
   Charge: SHADOW SHROUD — larger, longer-lasting smoke from the same
           bomb, granting stronger concealment / ambush opportunity.

3) SHADOW STEP
   Tap: Briefly teleport behind one marked foe and backstab.
   Charge: ASSASSINATION STEP — a longer aimed shadow step to a marked
           foe, with multiple coordinated stabs and short re-vanish.
   Do not auto-hit enemies beyond the explicit marked targeting area.

4) RAZOR WIRE (replaces Blade Fan)
   Tap: Throw a hooked wire that anchors and damages/slows enemies
        crossing its short line.
   Charge: RAZOR WEB — longer, denser multi-wire layout from the same
           wire trap, with bleeding effects. Visually indicate wires.

5) VENOM FLASK
   Tap: Throw one toxic flask leaving a poison puddle.
   Charge: TOXIC DELUGE — a larger/stronger flask that bursts into
           several connected poison pools over a wider area.

Thief anti-duplication: Keep Shadow Flurry as its distinct HOLD-ATK
special; avoid making all slots another knife fan or another toxic cloud.

6. CLASS-SPECIFIC SKILL TREE REDESIGN
======================================================================

Keep the existing shared Offense / Defense / Utility progression and
repeatable Training as progression foundations, but shift class mastery to
THREE BRANCHING, MEANINGFUL SPECIALIZATIONS per class. Avoid a mandatory
single six-node chain that simply buffs both starting spells.

SWORDSMAN
  VANGUARD: Braced Guard -> Shield Counter -> Last Stand -> Unyielding.
    Identity: defense, counters, crushing shield retaliation.
  DUELIST: Precision Window -> Combo Momentum -> Flawless Sequence -> Final Cut.
    Identity: timing, combo streaks, precision finishers.
  BERSERKER: Deep Wounds -> Battle Hunger -> Blood Frenzy -> Crimson Fury.
    Identity: bleed, aggression, calculated low-HP risk.

WIZARD
  ELEMENTALIST: Fire enhancement -> Cold control -> Elemental reaction
    -> Cataclysm. Reward combining fire/ice rather than repeating AoE.
  CRYSTAL ARTILLERY: Deep Piercing -> Splinterburst -> Glacial Pin
    -> Absolute Zero. Strong synergy with Icicle Cannon.
  ARCANIST: Mana Economy -> Orb Control -> Shield Resonance -> Arcane
    Fortress Mastery. Reward positioning, mana, and protective magic.

NECROMANCER
  BONEMASTER: Reinforced Skeletons -> Specialized Minion Attacks ->
    Guardian Control -> Bone Commander. Stronger FEWER summons, not spam.
  PLAGUEWEAVER: Contagion -> Corpse Catalyst -> Withering Infection ->
    Black Plague. Spread and area damage via rot/curses.
  SOUL REAPER: Greater Drain -> Soul Harvest -> Dangerous Sacrifice ->
    Soul Devourer. Life/mana conversion and finishing power.

THIEF
  ASSASSIN: Marked Target -> Backstab Precision -> Perfect Timing ->
    Perfect Execution. Critical precision and resets earned on kills.
  TRICKSTER: Smoke Veil -> Decoy -> Evasive Positioning -> Vanishing Act.
    Tactical stealth rather than raw damage.
  VENOMBLADE: Poisoned Blades -> Lingering Toxins -> Debilitating Venom
    -> Toxic Rupture. Damage-over-time and tactical detonation.

REQUIREMENTS FOR ALL CLASS TREES:
  - Meaningful visual/gameplay payoff at key tiers, especially capstones.
  - Specialize early; deeper branches unlock with level AND point investment.
  - Players can hybridize later; no accidental hard-lock to one branch.
  - Name and describe effects accurately (what changes, how long, how often).
  - Most upgrades should affect BOTH regular and charged variants in
    connected but individually appropriate ways.
  - Remove duplicate abilities/passives that only repeat one another.
  - Keep numerical Training for high-level points, to level 99.
  - Maintain balance against bosses, elite enemies and multiplayer/co-op.

7. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
======================================================================

A. ABILITY DATA MODEL
   - Give every active ability a reliable unique ID and paired tap/charge
     definition (cast function, targeting shape, cost, cooldown, VFX,
     effect upgrades and gameplay flags).
   - Preserve existing IDs where practical, especially the Wizard's
     legacy 'bolt' slot, so saved skill ranks/slots are not destroyed.
   - Update button/help descriptions so each ability explicitly shows
     both normal and charged effects.

B. INPUT/CHARGE ROUTING
   - Extend spell_charge.js (or refactor equivalent) for ALL equipped
     abilities in slots 1-4, not only spell1 and spell2.
   - Ensure actions go through slot->equipped ability->tap or charged
     dispatcher consistently for mouse, keyboard, touch and multiplayer.
   - Preserve existing charge-dash and HOLD-ATK systems independently.
   - Apply a SHARED cooldown per regular spell + charged version.

C. PROJECTILE / EFFECT IMPLEMENTATION
   - Use visible actual projectiles whenever the spell promises physical
     objects (icicles, knives, fireballs, bone spears, wire throws).
   - Maintain wall collision, enemy collision, status resistance,
     hit de-duplication, correct direction and range.
   - Avoid visual-only effects that suggest projectiles but apply
     unrelated instant damage in a hidden radius.
   - For Icicle Cannon specifically, use per-projectile unique hits,
     limited splash, sprite-or-canvas projectiles, embedded-shard visuals,
     small impact fragments and safe VFX cleanup.

D. SAVE MIGRATION
   - Old slots, ranks, flags, class perks and cooldowns should be
     migrated or safely defaulted without deleting players' progress.
   - Map or refund points spent on removed or renamed skills.
   - For Chain Lightning->Icicle Cannon, keep a compatible stable ID and
     map old generic Power/Haste/Reach ranks where sensible, while
     replacing obsolete Chain/Forked status/ranks with a new equivalent
     or a one-time refunded skill point.
   - Run migration only once. Never repeatedly refund on reload.

E. BALANCING / UX
   - Ensure stronger charged versions do not make tap useless. Tap
     should remain a fast, efficient, lower-cost combat choice.
   - Reward thoughtful aiming/positioning rather than unavoidable AoE.
   - Keep attack naming, effects, damage and animation synchronized.
   - Charge UI must be clear on small phone screens and readable through
     the Lady of the Pond fog/other layered effects.
   - No sustained freeze-frame on every crit; preserve existing
     critical-kill animation settings and hit-stop rules.

F. PERFORMANCE
   - Cap simultaneous projectiles, attached shards, chain explosions,
     particle counts and temporary summons.
   - Prefer shared sprites/particle pools over expensive per-shot allocations.
   - No unbounded timer callbacks or enemy references that persist
     after death/zone changes.
   - Verify steady performance on mid-range Android phones and desktop.

8. ACCEPTANCE TESTS / DEFINITION OF DONE
======================================================================

1. EVERY one of the 20 active abilities has a working tap and a working
   charged variant in every allowed equipment slot.
2. Holding a button >0.25s shows a relevant preview; short taps do not.
3. Charged attack is an obviously powered-up version of the tap ability.
4. Every spell has consistent mana/cooldown behavior with NO accidental
   extra tap available immediately after a charge release.
5. Regular Icicle Cannon fires ONE actual ice projectile with at most
   THREE unique direct hits. Each hit produces a small capped splash.
6. Charged Icicle Cannon fires multiple individually visible physical
   icicles from the staff in a FORWARD AIMED CONE, with staggered volleys.
7. Charged icicles visibly EMBED in enemies, track their movement, and
   shatter/fall away after a short delay or on death.
8. Bosses cannot be frozen indefinitely, and large hitboxes do not
   multiply icicle damage without limit.
9. Frost Nova charged remains a radial nova, not a second ice cannon.
10. Swordsman has only one main spin-themed regular/charged ability pair;
    the rest have distinct roles and effects.
11. Necromancer active abilities do not repeatedly produce separate
    skeleton armies; one summon path remains meaningful.
12. Thief wires/traps are usable and visually readable; knife and venom
    skills each have differentiated mechanics.
13. New class skill branches can be bought, display correct costs/ranks,
    and change both tap and charged variants as described.
14. Existing saves load safely with correct investments/slots, with
    one-time compatibility migration/refunds.
15. Mobile thumb-drag aiming works in all directions, especially SE.
16. Co-op clients see consistent spell visuals without duplicate damage.
17. Repeated casts/area changes/death/restarts cause no leaked projectiles,
    attached-ice sprites, summoned units, timers or leftover preview rings.
18. Existing automated tests pass; add specific tests for charging every
    unlocked ability, icicle piercing/splash/caps, cooldown integration,
    save migration, and ability-specific status effects.

  Playtest all four classes, mobile & PC, dense combat, bosses, co-op,

FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE:
A charged spell should never surprise the player by becoming a different
ability. It is the SAME spell, amplified in a satisfying, readable way.
Icicle Cannon must especially feel like firing an actual ice weapon:
one very sharp piercing icicle on tap, or a fierce cone of distinct icy
projectiles on charge, visibly stabbing and sticking into enemies.
